Challenging the assumptions

could be lowerVacancy may be shorter than we think

The space is a fully fitted, second-generation restaurant with a patio by the arenas. Operators are converting existing restaurants to avoid build-out costs, and the Bier Markt site down the street was re-let (to us, as Dolly's) within months. A landlord will argue 4–6 months, not 12.

could be higher…but the pool for a 5,600 sq ft box is thin

Demand is strongest for units under 2,000 sq ft. 41% of operators are losing money or breaking even, and Ontario restaurant insolvencies are up 7.5%. This would also be the second restaurant to fail here since 2024. Large asks nearby sit at $35–42.

may be zeroBroker fees aren't a given

We signed this lease without a broker, about $150K saved on the commission. The landlord can lease direct again. Set commissions to 0% to test it.

market checkMarket rent is above our proposal, below the lease

Dolly's signed at about $35 net down the street in 2026. Size-comparable asks are $35–42. Our proposal starts at $31.35 and reaches $48.06 by Year 10. The lease runs $47–64. So the landlord's real loss from the proposal is the gap to market: about $9/sq ft in Year 2, shrinking to under $1 by Year 10 (see the rent table), not the full $15.68.

lower than you'd thinkTenant improvements are small here

Our kitchen, bar and dining room stay with the building. A new restaurant needs $10–30/sq ft, not the $75–150 a shell needs. That's a real benefit to the landlord of us leaving behind a fitted space.

benefit to landlordEquipment, deposit, and a claim against us

Abandoned chattels can be sold, but at 5–15% of cost after fees and liens. The $48,866 deposit is applied. A damages claim against Esplanade Cocktail Company Ltd. (no guarantor in the lease) recovers little after legal fees. Equipment under lease or financing goes back to its owner.

landlord's biggest issueA lower face rent lowers the building's value

A permanent $88.6K/yr cut is worth about $1.5M less at a 5–6% cap rate. This matters to their lender and on any sale or refinance. A rent abatement or rebate that keeps the face rent at the lease schedule avoids it, and is the structure most likely to get a yes.

other landlord costsDark space next to condo owners

Insurance on a vacant unit, base utilities, security, condo-board pressure, lender covenant tests, and no vacancy tax rebate (ended in Toronto in 2018). Additional rent of $28.84/sq ft, about $163K a year, falls on the landlord while the space is empty.

landlord benefits of saying yesA proven operator that's growing

Sales are up 29% year over year, with no downtime, no capital outlay and no leasing risk. A new restaurant tenant has its own failure risk (the "new-tenant failure risk" lever).

risk to our sideWhat the landlord can do instead of re-leasing

Under Highway Properties a landlord can keep the lease alive and sue for rent as it falls due. Ontario's Court of Appeal (Aphria, 2024) says there is no duty to mitigate in that case, and the Supreme Court's ruling is pending. Against a single-purpose company with no guarantor, collecting is the hard part. Confirm with counsel; this is not legal advice.
